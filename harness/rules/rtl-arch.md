# RTL Rules (Branch: fpint_naive — MMIO job descriptor + internal GEMM FSM)

- Do not assume `VX_cmd_constructor.sv`, `VX_instruction_if.sv`, `VX_gemm_job_frontend.sv`, or `VX_gemm_tmem_dma_ctrl.sv` exists in this branch. Those belong to the `fpint_improve` instruction-stream flow.
- The software-visible interface is the MMIO job descriptor handled by `hw/rtl/core/VX_job_frontend.sv` and consumed by `VX_gemm_ctrl.sv` / `VX_gemm_fsm.sv`.
- `gemm_unified_cmd_t` in `VX_gpu_pkg.sv` is an internal command payload generated by `VX_gemm_fsm.sv` and routed through `VX_gemm_sync.sv`; do not treat it as a software instruction-stream ABI.
- DMA/MXU command details are derived inside RTL from job registers, tile constants, and effective edge sizes. If changing address, stride, bound, or segment-size behavior, update the relevant mapping in `VX_gemm_fsm.sv`, `VX_gemm_node.sv`, and `VX_gemm_dma_ctrl.sv` together.
- Keep `VX_gemm_sync.sv` wait/notify register semantics consistent with the command sequence emitted by `VX_gemm_fsm.sv`.
