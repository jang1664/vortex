# C4 SRAM attention: M0–M3 requirement and evidence audit

Assessment updated: 2026-10-24. This consolidates the [implementation plan](../../docs/future_optim/c4_flash_attention_tmem_lmem_dma_plan.md), current source and completed verification receipts. The consolidation itself changed no production files or tests; a subsequent explicitly assigned seeded-payload verification ran independently, and its completed receipt is incorporated below. The [before snapshot](logs/audit_consolidation/m0-m3-evidence-audit.before.md) preserves the original findings and subsequent appendices.

**Conclusion:** The named functional M0–M3 contracts now have completed evidence for queued producer publication, physical completion, resident I/O, reset with pending bridge work, sequence protection, validation, multi-tile online attention, empty-prefix workers and early kernel return. The table below distinguishes that evidence from the remaining physical and end-to-end gates. The broad attention matrix retains its actual source revisions; it was not all rerun after the local-owner repair. The final M1 randomized-payload requirement is also covered by the independently executed six-case seeded supplement, retaining all original patterns and randomized stalls. No general all-configurations, all-interleavings or arbitrary-stale-packet proof is required or claimed.
